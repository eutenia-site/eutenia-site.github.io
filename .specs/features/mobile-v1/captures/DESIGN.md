## Brand & Style

This design system channels visceral momentum, atmospheric depth, and raw stage presence. Engineered for the heavy music landscape, it marries cinematic brutality with the precision and polish of luxury high-performance interfaces.

### Brand Identity & Personality
- **Atmospheric & Heavy:** Built on crushing negative space, deep stage shadows, and stark focal points that echo intense post-hardcore breakdowns and heavy riffs.
- **Aggressive yet Refined:** Moves past dated, chaotic grunge tropes into a curated, hyper-polished metal aesthetic: crisp metallic boundaries, subtle directional red blooms, and surgical typographic discipline.
- **Emotional Impact:** Evokes tension, anticipation, adrenaline, and belonging—placing the listener front-row at a darkened arena before the floodlights hit.

### Design Movement & Influence
- **Cinematic Neo-Brutalism:** Monolithic condensed typography anchored by sharp structural lines, high-contrast borders, and deep layered carbon surfaces.
- **Chiaroscuro Atmosphere:** Radical lighting contrast with dark slate surfaces punctured by crimson volumetric glows, ambient laser vignettes, and brushed chrome highlights.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising dark-mode architecture. Deep voids provide the stage, while surgical red accents command focus and metallic neutrals preserve tactical clarity.

### Color Tiers & Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#0a0b0e` - Onyx Void):** The deepest backdrop level. Used for master screen backdrops and hero lockups.
- **Surface Elevation (`#14161d` - Charcoal Matrix):** Secondary container surface for cards, media players, flyouts, and tabular tour rows.
- **Surface Highlight (`#1d212b` - Slate Steel):** Active hover states, interactive table rows, and raised drawer headers.
- **Primary Accent (`#ff2a3b` - Blood Surge):** High-voltage crimson reserved for destructive actions, VIP callouts, live tour date indicators, audio play heads, and primary conversion pathways.
- **Secondary Metallic (`#8e95a5` - Anodized Chrome):** Structural dividers, subdued metadata, audio scrubber tracks, and unselected tab headers.
- **Tertiary Pure Chrome (`#f1f3f7` - Flash Silver):** High-luminance headline text, high-priority readouts, and bright state icons.

### Application Guidelines
- Crimson red must never exceed 10-15% of the overall screen viewport; it acts as a laser sight, guiding immediate action without saturating the visual space.
- Subtle inner glows (`rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.15)`) accompany active/playing items rather than solid, flat red fills.

## Typography

The typography orchestrates high dynamic contrast between structural impact and technical clarity.

### Family Assignments
- **Display & Headlines (`Oswald`):** Condensed, commanding, and monolithic. All Oswald headlines and UI tags must be set in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.02em` to `0.12em`) to mirror festival poster bills, stage production stamps, and industrial gear badging.
- **Body & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, modern, highly legible sans-serif. Counters the aggressive punch of Oswald, ensuring liner notes, tour venue details, merchandise specs, and purchase flows read clearly across high-density layouts.

### Implementation Rules
- Always render `label-*` and `display-*` roles in uppercase.
- For headline line heights, maintain tight ratios (`1.0` to `1.1`) to emphasize dense, compressed typographic lockups.
- Avoid low-contrast body text; body copy relies on `#8e95a5` for secondary passages and `#f1f3f7` for primary narrative text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid with precise, controlled negative space that anchors large focal assets (music videos, merch hero imagery, stage photography).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, `margin-lg: 4rem` outer margin, `gutter-lg: 2rem` gap. Maximum content constraint at 1440px with centered margin distribution.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem` outer margin, `gutter: 1.5rem` gap.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm: 1rem` outer margin, `gutter-sm: 1rem` gap.

### Vertical Spacing & Rhythm
- Major section transitions (e.g., Tour Dates to Discography, Merch showcase to Video player) use massive structural gaps of `space-xl` scaled dynamically (up to `5rem` on desktop) to evoke cinematic breathing room.
- Component-internal spacing strictly adopts the four-point multiples defined in `space-xs` through `space-md` for dense, tactical grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined by dark tonal layering, laser-thin metallic borders, and calibrated light emission rather than diffuse ambient drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas Void):** `#0a0b0e` — Canvas background. Flat, infinite.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** `#14161d` — Standard panel baseline. Finished with a 1px structural stroke of `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals & Flyouts):** `#1d212b` — Floating media controls, navigation overlays, cart drawers. Uses a 1px border of `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.25)` and a sharp shadow: `0 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.

### Crimson Radiation & Glows
- Interactive focal elements (such as play heads, active tour badges, or primary purchase triggers) emit a calibrated directional light:
  - **Subtle Accent Glow:** `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.28);`
  - **Overdrive State (Active/Hover):** `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.55), inset 0 0 8px rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.2);`

### Border Philosophy
Avoid soft blurry boundaries. Emphasize razor-thin 1px metallic borders with low opacity (`10% - 25%`) to mimic milled brushed hardware and flight-case touring gear.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sharp geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

### Geometric Rationale
- Heavy music, industrial staging, and modern brutalist sensibilities rely on uncompromising 90-degree corners.
- Cards, persistent media bars, button housings, and input fields all use zero-radius framing.
- The sole exception to the zero-radius rule belongs to **Badge Pills** and **Track Playhead Dots**, which adopt dedicated pill/circle geometries to distinguish runtime status and metadata from structural interactive targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Bloodline Action):** Sharp 0px corners, background `#ff2a3b`, label color `#f1f3f7`, font `label-lg`. Hover expands visual presence via `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.45)` and shifts background to `#e50914`. Active state steps down brightness slightly.
- **Secondary (Metallic Armor):** Background `transparent`, 1px border in `#8e95a5`, text `#f1f3f7`, font `label-lg`. Hover transitions background to `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.1)` and border to `#f1f3f7`.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent background, no border, text `#8e95a5`. Hover shifts text to `#ff2a3b` with 150ms ease.

### Badges & Tour Status Pills
- Compact, uppercase metadata badges displaying tour statuses (`SOLD OUT`, `LOW TICKETS`, `VIP AVAILABLE`, `LATEST RELEASE`).
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font `label-sm`.
- **Sold Out:** Background `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.15)`, text `#8e95a5`, border `1px solid rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.3)`.
- **Low Tickets / VIP:** Background `rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.15)`, text `#ff2a3b`, border `1px solid #ff2a3b`.

### Tour Dates List Component
- Row-based list system. Each date is an edge-to-edge interactive card on surface `#14161d` bordered with `1px solid rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.12)`.
- Column layout: Date & Month (`Oswald` 1.5rem, `#f1f3f7`), City & Venue (`Plus Jakarta Sans` 1rem with subtitle), Tour Badges, and Action Button (`GET TICKETS`).
- On row hover, border transitions to `rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.5)` with a left edge crimson indicator tab (`3px solid #ff2a3b`).

### Audio & Music Player Widget
- Persistent bottom docking or inline module. Surface `#14161d`, top border `1px solid rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.25)`.
- **Waveform / Scrubber Track:** Background `#1d212b`, active fill `#ff2a3b` with a terminal glowing knob.
- **Controls:** Master play button is a 48px sharp square or circular glyph with glowing state, flanked by silver track navigation arrows.

### Merchandise Cards
- Sharp rectangular frame with a fixed 1:1 or 4:5 image container. Background `#14161d`, subtle border `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.12)`.
- Image zooms slightly (`scale(1.03)`) under an inner vignette upon hover.
- Footer displays item title in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold, currency readout in `Oswald` Bold (`#f1f3f7`), and quick "PRE-ORDER" / "ADD" slide-up button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Dark field `#0a0b0e`, 1px border `#8e95a5`, text `#f1f3f7`, caret `#ff2a3b`. Focus state triggers `border-color: #ff2a3b` and `box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(255, 42, 59, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square box (`18px x 18px`), dark interior `#0a0b0e`, border `#8e95a5`. Checked state fills `#ff2a3b` with a `#f1f3f7` angled tick icon.

### Navigation Header
- Fixed / sticky top navigation. Background `rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and bottom 1px separator `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.15)`.
- Monolithic brand mark left-aligned; navigation links styled in `label-lg`, tracking `0.1em`, transitioning from `#8e95a5` to `#f1f3f7` with red underline indicators on active selection.
